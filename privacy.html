Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This notice explains how Cakes by Sharn uses information you provide when making an enquiry or arranging an order through www.cakesbysharn.co.uk.

Who handles your information?

Cakes by Sharn is responsible for information collected through this website. For questions about your personal data, to update your details or to make a privacy request, please email cakesbysharnx@hotmail.com.

What information do we collect?

When you submit an enquiry, we collect your name, email address and UK mobile number; the products or custom requirements you request; your preferred date; collection or delivery preference; and your delivery address if applicable. We also record the reference, date and status of your enquiry, agreed order details and communications connected with your request. Administrators may record payment references and related business records. Do not submit payment-card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information in an enquiry.

Why do we use it?

  • To respond to your enquiry, check availability, agree details and arrange your order. This is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract and to perform an agreed order.
  • To send service messages such as enquiry acknowledgments, approval details or a decline explanation. These are transactional messages, not marketing subscriptions.
  • To maintain appropriate business, accounting and audit records where necessary to comply with legal obligations and to run and protect our business.
  • To help prevent abuse of the enquiry service and protect customer information, based on legitimate business interests.

Providing your name, email address and mobile number is required to submit an enquiry through this website. An enquiry is not a confirmed order until we agree it with you.

Who receives the information?

Customer enquiries and order details are handled in our private administration system, hosted using Supabase. We use Resend for transactional emails when the sending service is configured. Our website is hosted with GitHub Pages. We do not sell your personal information or use the enquiry form to subscribe you to marketing.

How long do we keep it?

Enquiry and order records are retained to handle requests, manage orders, resolve queries and meet accounting or other legal recordkeeping requirements. The system does not currently apply an automatic fixed deletion date to all customer records. You can contact us to request review, correction or deletion of information that is no longer necessary, subject to legal requirements to keep certain records.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access to your data, correction of inaccuracies, deletion, restriction of processing, or object to certain uses. You can contact us using the email address above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use customer information, we will update this page and the last-updated date.
