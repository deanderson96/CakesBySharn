Your details

For a custom cake, describe what you need in the requirements box instead of selecting an item.

Sharn will review the final price and date before approving an order. An on-screen reference confirms receipt. Automatic acknowledgment emails are not available yet; our team will respond manually by email.

When submitting online, your name, email, order details and any delivery address are sent to our enquiry service and used to respond and manage your order. We do not use these details for marketing. Contact cakesbysharnx@hotmail.com with questions about your information.

Submitting an enquiry does not confirm an order. Our team will review availability and final pricing, then contact you using your supplied email address.

Allergies or dietary requirements? Include these in your message and ask Sharn for ingredient and allergen information before ordering.
